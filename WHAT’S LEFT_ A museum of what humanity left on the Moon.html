Why these objects matter

A mission does not end when the crew leaves. Mirrors placed in 1969 still give scientists measurements today. A network of instruments ran far longer than planned, then was switched off because of priorities and funding. Each object holds a decision about what to keep, what to stop, and what to leave behind.

WHAT’S LEFT? starts with the Moon, where the evidence is closest and the records are best.

NASA Acknowledgment

WHAT'S LEFT? was inspired by NASA's exploration of the Moon and the human-made equipment left behind during lunar missions. This project uses publicly available NASA resources and 3D assets to preserve the history of space exploration and raise awareness of humanity's lasting presence beyond Earth.
